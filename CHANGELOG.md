## [1.0.0+26.3] - 2026-09-27

### Added

- When an item you hold or wear wears down to 10 durability or less, it is moved to your inventory, like a shift-click.
- If your inventory is full, the item is dropped on the ground instead.
- A worn item held in the main hand can no longer mine or hit, so it cannot break that way.
- Client-side only: works in singleplayer and on any server, including vanilla ones.
